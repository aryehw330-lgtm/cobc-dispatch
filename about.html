Laser Name Helper

Laser Name Helper is a tool for running fast, organized sign-ups at in-person laser engraving events - the kind where a line of people each pick an item, a color, and a font, and want their name or a word engraved on the spot.

What it does

  • A simple sign-up page guests fill out on a tablet or their phone - no app to install, no account to create
  • Sign-ups are organized into a live queue, grouped by item and color, so blanks can be batched instead of swapped one at a time
  • A companion desktop tool reads the queue and can automatically switch fonts in xTool Studio to match each person's request
  • Guests can check their spot in line from their own phone without needing to ask

Where the data lives

Every event's sign-up data is stored in the organizer's own Google Sheet - see the Privacy Policy for details on exactly what is collected and how it is used, or the Terms of Service.
