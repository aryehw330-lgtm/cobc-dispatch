COBC Dispatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

COBC Dispatch is the private call and dispatch app of COBC Bergen County Chaverim, a volunteer organization that helps people with roadside and other emergencies. The app is for COBC members only; you need a COBC unit number and PIN to sign in. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app uses

  • Member details: name, unit number, phone number and role (member, dispatcher, admin), from COBC's member list.
  • Call details: when someone calls COBC for help, dispatchers record what is needed to send help — the caller's name and phone number, the address or location of the call, vehicle details, and notes.
  • Location while responding: when a member responds to a call and chooses to share it, the member's live location is shown to the dispatchers and members working that call, so the call can be coordinated. It is not tracked outside of calls.
  • Call activity: who responded to which call, call times and outcomes, used for dispatching and for COBC's internal statistics.
  • Notification ID: a device code that lets the app send call alerts to your phone.

How it is used

Only to run COBC's dispatch: sending call alerts, coordinating responders, keeping the dispatch schedule, and keeping records of calls. The app has no ads and does not use information for advertising or marketing.

Who can see it

Information is shown only to COBC members, according to their role. Call details, including the caller's name and phone number, are shared with COBC members so they can respond — in the app and in COBC's members-only WhatsApp group. Some screens (such as caller history and admin tools) are limited to dispatchers and admins. COBC does not sell or rent any information.

Where it is stored

Information is stored with service providers that COBC uses to run the app: Google (Firebase, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, which also deliver notifications) and Cloudflare. Call announcements are also posted to COBC's members-only WhatsApp group. All information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

How long it is kept

Call records are kept for COBC's records and statistics. Live locations are only used while a call is active. When a member leaves COBC, their access is turned off.

Your choices

  • You can turn call alerts off in the app's Settings, or in your phone's settings.
  • Location sharing only happens when you allow it on your phone.
  • To ask about or correct your information, or to ask for it to be removed, contact COBC below.

Children

The app is for adult COBC members and is not directed to children.
